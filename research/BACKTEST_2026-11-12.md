# SPY Monster Day Detector — Robinhood Backtest (November 12, 2026)

**Research status: not validated.** All prices below are SPY **underlying**, not 0DTE option premiums. No brokerage orders are placed by this project.

## Data and coverage

- Connected Robinhood 5-minute regular-session OHLCV for SPY, QQQ, and IWM.
- 184 full 78-candle sessions from **February 23 to November 12, 2026**.
- The first 20 complete trading sessions establish the lookback median, leaving **164** eligible sessions.
- Earlier 2025 and January 2026 5-minute requests returned interpolated/gap-filled bars rather than actual market activity; these were excluded. The year-long *daily*-bar review is separate from this ~9-month intraday backtest.
- Historical confirmation uses contemporaneous QQQ and IWM movements from each day's regular-session open and SPY's volume-weighted typical-price VWAP.
- Robinhood's daily and intraday high for November 4 disagree ($775.64 vs. $769.22). The backtest uses 5-minute candles consistently.

## Mechanical rules (one signal per trading day)

**Opening shock (first 30 minutes):** after the 9:55–10:00 ET candle closes, require first-30-minute SPY high/low range at least **1.5×** the preceding 20 sessions' median first-30-minute range, 30-minute cumulative SPY volume at least **1.5×** its corresponding median, opening-to-current close at least **60%** of opening range directionally, and SPY close versus VWAP plus same-direction QQQ and IWM movement.

**Developing expansion:** if no shock, scan 10:05–14:00 ET completed 5-minute candles for a close outside the 9:30–10:00 SPY high/low, same-day cumulative SPY range at least **1.25×** prior-20-session median range achieved at that same candle index, volume at least **1.2×** same-time cumulative median, absolute open-to-close movement at least **35%** of range, and aligned QQQ, IWM, and VWAP direction.

**Execution proxy:** hypothetical position opens on the *next* 5-minute candle at its open, with **$0.05 per SPY share unfavorable entry slippage**. Initial stop distance = clamp(0.35 × prior-20-day median daily high-low range, $1.50, $3.00). Stop is intrabar, with conservative stop-first priority if both stop and first target are touched in one candle. Half the position is sold at **2R**; the remainder trails **1.5R below/above the highest favorable price seen, effective the *next* candle**, with a final exit near 4 PM. Exits incur a further **$0.05 per SPY share unfavorable slippage**, or worse if a bar opens through a stop. No margin, interest, dividends, options pricing, implied-volatility changes, bid-ask spreads in an option, liquidity limits, brokerage commissions, latency, or taxes. Returns in **R units of SPY price risk**, *not dollars or options returns*.

No parameters are selected to maximize profit within this report. Nevertheless, these rules were designed after examining November 5; even the later-period July–November split is **not a genuinely untouched validation set**. Further fully prospective paper evaluation is required.

Defining a "large" day as same-day high-low >= **1.5× preceding 20-session median daily range**, 27 of 164 days were large, and the combined detector flagged 17. Only 17 of the 41 flagged days later met the large-day threshold; many alerts were false positives. Among the five **$15+** high-low days in the eligible sample, the combined detector flagged four. Among the two **$20+** days, it flagged two. This classification is known only **after** the day finishes; it is not a forecast.

### November 5 illustrative trade

At approximately 10:00, SPY had already made a **$13.14** first-half-hour range, around **6.2×** the prior-20 first-30 median, with volume about **2.85×** its prior-20 median. QQQ, IWM, and SPY-versus-VWAP all agreed in the bearish direction. In the *underlying-only* proxy, the detector opened a short-bias position at approximately **$754.09** at 10:00, used about a **$2.12 SPY-price-point** initial stop, and exited at **+2.43R** under the partial-plus-trailing rules. **That does not mean an SPY 0DTE put would earn +2.43R.** Real option fills, Greeks, and the contract choice may change the result dramatically.

### Why a $33 day doesn't guarantee big trading profit

The stop, intraday counter-moves, and option pricing often prevent capturing the entire day's range. Another example, June 9, had a **$24.31** high-low range yet the tested trend entry was stopped out at roughly **−1.02R**. Big realized daily ranges and executable profit are distinct.

## Live monitor behavior

The new `/monster.html` UI is **research-only**. The matching `/api/monster` Cloudflare Pages Function obtains SPY history plus current QQQ and IWM bars from the user's already-configured private market-data provider. It requires **20 complete prior sessions**; no baseline means no signal. It uses the **same thresholds** as this research, only completed 5-minute candles, and does not invent missing bars.

Tradier's regular-session 5-minute time-sales data can cover up to ~40 days according to its API documentation, sufficient in ordinary conditions for a 20-session median. Free Alpaca IEX is *single-exchange only*, so the endpoint deliberately **suppresses research alerts** from that source. The original `/api/spy` endpoint remains unchanged.

Alerts are **in-browser only** and the page must remain open and visible. No persistent cloud monitoring, push notifications, options executions, broker account mutations, or orders.

## Before considering live trading

1. Run the detector in paper mode for substantially more independent observations, including volatile and quiet markets.
2. Use consolidated and appropriately licensed SPY/QQQ/IWM quotes, and historical *options* bid/ask/IV for contract-specific simulations.
3. Stress-test different stops, late signals, gap risk, order latency, spread and adverse selection; don't tune and report on the same data.
4. Enforce a firm option-premium risk cap and one-loss/day discipline independently of the chart-level invalidation.
5. Only promote rules after robust prospective results. A negative backtest is evidence **against** blindly following the signal.
